# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

This privacy policy applies to **Lifebot**, a personal integration application that connects to your Oura account.

## What this app does

Lifebot is a **personal, single-user** productivity dashboard. It reads data from your Oura account — specifically sleep, readiness, and activity scores — and displays it alongside other productivity information on your own local machine.

## Personal use only

This app is built and run for one person: the app owner. It is not a commercial product, is not distributed to others, and has no other users.

## Data we collect and store

- **Oura data (read-only):** Lifebot requests read access to your Oura data via the Oura API. It only reads this data; it never writes to, modifies, or deletes anything in your Oura account.
- **Storage:** Data retrieved from Oura is stored only on the app owner's local machine. It is not uploaded to any external server, cloud service, or database owned by third parties.

## What we do NOT do

- ❌ We do **not** sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
- ❌ We do **not** use your data for advertising or analytics of any kind.
- ❌ We do **not** transfer Oura data to any external service.
- ❌ We do **not** store credit card, payment, or other sensitive personal information.

## Access to your Oura account

The app uses Oura's official API with OAuth-style authorization. Access is limited to the scope needed to read your daily readiness, sleep, and activity data. You can revoke this access at any time from your Oura account settings.

## Changes to this policy

Since this is a personal integration, this policy is updated only as needed. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Please contact the app owner directly.
